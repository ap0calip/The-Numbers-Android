Privacy Policy for "The Numbers"

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

The Numbers ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you download and use the app.

🔒 Summary: "The Numbers" does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All progress and settings remain 100% private and local to your device.

1. Information Collection and Use

We believe in complete user privacy, especially for educational and learning tools. When you use The Numbers:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.
  • No Network Transmission: The application operates fully offline. No user data, usage analytics, or activity logs are transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers.
  • Local Data Storage: Your practice preferences, quiz high scores, best results, and unlocked reward stickers are saved strictly locally on your device using an embedded local database. This data never leaves your device and can be permanently removed at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app data in your device settings.

2. Children's Privacy

The Numbers is designed to provide an engaging, safe, and intuitive arithmetic learning experience for users of all ages, including children.

  • We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy requirements.
  • We do NOT collect any personal information from children or any other users.
  • The app contains NO advertisements, NO in-app purchases, NO social media plugins, and NO links to external websites.

3. Device Permissions and Features

The application only utilizes core device system capabilities necessary for its interactive features:

  • Text-to-Speech (TTS): Uses the built-in Android system Text-to-Speech service to pronounce numbers aloud when tapped. No voice recordings are stored or processed outside your device.
  • Audio / Sound Effects: Uses local audio playback for feedback during quizzes and practice sessions.
  • Permissions: The application does not require or request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, or External Storage.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We do not integrate any third-party SDKs, tracking tools, advertising networks, or analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or AdMob).

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
